# Minimum triangle derivations in the arithmetic counterexample

**The full Paley conjecture and Proximity Prize remain unproved.**
This note classifies the 119 six-term remainder orbits in the fixed field
from [pass 30](parallel30-triangle-remainder-2026-09-05.md). It proves
minimum lengths of derivations using the field's zero-sum triangles.
It supplies no uniform upper bound on the remainder and no improved
cancellation exponent. No literature novelty or Lean verification is claimed.

The field and subgroup are

    p=215535361, n=128, g=25525303, H=<g>.

There is one scaling orbit of distinct zero-sum triangles, represented by
A={1,g,g^19}. Every zero-sum polynomial relation belongs to the principal
ideal generated by f=1+X+X^19 in R=Z[X]/(X^64+1). Both facts were checked
in pass30: kappa=6 and 2 is not in H; multiplication by f has determinant p.

The new result is that 45 of the 65 primitive six-term orbits require
38--44 scaled triangle equations to leave their six entries after
opposite-pair cancellation. This is a minimum, not merely the length
returned by a particular expansion algorithm.

## 1. What a triangle derivation means

Use formal symbols for the elements g^j, with only the identifications
[g^(j+64)]=-[g^j]. The resulting free abelian group is the additive group
of R. A six-set S of distinct, opposite-free elements has formal vector

    F_S(X)=sum_(g^e in S) X^e mod (X^64+1).

Its canonical degree-below-64 vector has six coefficients equal to +1
or -1. This formal vector is retained; merely evaluating it to zero in
F_p would erase the counting problem.

A triangle derivation is a multiset of actual scaled zero-sum triangles
tA, t in H, followed by cancellation of opposite entries until precisely
S remains. Negative uses of a triangle are already allowed, since -1
is in H and -tA is another scaled triangle. Repetitions are allowed.

Writing t=g^j, the formal vector of tA is X^j f. Thus a derivation
with L triangle terms gives F_S=fq in R, where q has an expression as
a sum of L signed monomials of degree below 64. Conversely, every such
expression for q gives a triangle derivation: after all opposite pairs
are canceled, the surviving formal vector is F_S and its entries are
exactly the distinct, opposite-free set S.

## 2. The unique quotient proves the minimum length

Multiplication by f is injective over Q and over Z because its determinant
is the nonzero integer p. It follows that F_S=fq has at most one q in R.
In this example it has an integral solution for every field relation,
because fR equals the evaluation kernel as proved in pass30.

Write that unique solution as q=sum_(j=0)^63 q_j X^j. Any expression
using L scaled triangles has L>=sum_j |q_j|: opposite monomials can
only decrease the absolute sum of the reduced coefficient vector.
The lower bound is attained by using |q_j| copies of g^j A when q_j>0,
and |q_j| copies of -g^j A when q_j<0. Therefore

    minimum number of triangle terms for S = ||q||_1.    (1)

Multiplying S by an element of H merely applies a signed cyclic shift
to q. Its absolute coefficient sum is unchanged. Thus (1) is an invariant
of the unordered six-set scaling orbit, independent of the normalization.
Choosing another representative of the same triangle orbit likewise
only shifts the quotient. No alternative choice of triangle scales or
repetitions can shorten the lengths certified below.

More generally, for one triangle orbit with a nonzero multiplication
determinant, this minimum statement applies whenever the rational quotient
F_S/f is integral. If it is not integral, that formal relation cannot
be generated by this triangle orbit at all. The determinant-p condition
in this particular field guarantees integrality for every zero relation;
that guarantee is not asserted for arbitrary fields or triangle families.

    S={0,8,16,45,62,100}

requires at least 38 triangle equations. Its unique quotient has +1

    {0,3,6,9,12,15,20,23,26,29,32,37,40,43,46,49,52,54,57,60}

and -1 coefficients on

    {2,5,10,13,19,22,25,27,30,33,36,39,42,47,50,53,56,59}.

All remaining coefficients are zero. Direct multiplication by
1+X+X^19 modulo X^64+1 gives 1+X^8+X^16-X^36+X^45+X^62,

This supplies a checkable certificate of the minimum 38 without having
to inspect the entire search. Every listed six-set is still a relation
inside the actual prime subgroup, not an abstract sign model.

For comparison, the primitive exponent set {0,1,10,36,82,120} has

    q=1-X^18+X^36-X^55,

so its minimum is four. The long quotients are therefore not a uniform
overhead of the chosen representation: they distinguish different
relations in the same field and triangle orbit.

## 4. Every minimum derivation of a primitive six-set needs cycles

For a minimum derivation with L triangle terms, make a graph with one
vertex per triangle copy and an edge for each canceled opposite pair.
Each vertex supplies three entries. Six entries survive, so every
complete pairing has exactly

    E=(3L-6)/2

edges. There are no loops, since a nonzero zero-sum triangle has no
internal opposite pair. Multiple triangle copies cause no difficulty.

Suppose the target six-set is primitive, meaning it has no proper
nonempty zero-sum subset. Then this graph is connected for every complete
pairing in a minimum derivation. To prove this, note that minimum length
means no coefficient position uses both a triangle and its negative;
otherwise the absolute-sum lower bound in (1) would be strict. Thus every
nonempty collection of vertices has a nonzero formal coefficient vector
u before multiplication by f. Injectivity of multiplication by f means
that such a collection cannot have zero formal boundary after cancellation.

A graph component therefore has a nonempty subset of the six surviving
entries as its boundary. Their sum in F_p is zero, because the component
was made from zero-sum triangles and internal opposite-pair cancellations.
Two or more components would produce a proper nonempty zero-sum subset
of the target, contradicting primitivity. This proves connectivity.

Consequently the cycle rank of every minimum cancellation graph is

    E-L+1 = L/2-2.                                      (2)

The twenty primitive orbits with minimum four have tree derivations.
The remaining forty-five require cycle rank 17, 18, 19 or 20. For a
nonminimum derivation, extra canceling triangle pairs can be inserted;
no invariant cycle-rank assertion is made for those padded derivations.

These graphs encode cancellation among triangle equations. They are
not being identified with the separate spectral necklace graphs or
with a new proof of a spectral bound.

## 5. Exact computation and verification

The [classifier](../experiments/parallel31_short_multiples_2026_09_05.py)
first constructs B in R with fB=p. It does this by exact quadratic norm
descent: if f(X)=e(X^2)+Xo(X^2), then
f(X)f(-X)=e(X^2)^2-X^2o(X^2)^2. The smaller-degree adjugate identity
lifts recursively. Each lifted identity is checked by direct integer
multiplication. No rational rounding or numerical linear solve is used.

For each F_S, the program forms F_SB, verifies every coefficient is
divisible by p, sets q=F_SB/p, and checks fq=F_S. It separately expands
the corresponding multiset of actual triangles and cancels their formal
opposites. The resulting entries agree with S. An explicit cancellation
graph is stored and its survivors, edge count, connectivity and cycle
rank are checked.

A [separate certificate checker](../experiments/parallel31_check_certificate_2026_09_05.py)
uses the row equations for multiplication by the trinomial, without the
classifier's generic multiplication or norm descent. It verifies the
integer inverse certificate, every quotient, field sum, and product
partition. It checks all proper subsets directly to confirm primitivity.
Its [record](../results/parallel31_certificate_check_2026_09_05.json)
also compares the orbit count with the earlier independent C++ total.
The classifier records 1,148 checks and this checker records 1,026.

The total of 119 orbits and the 54/65 split agree with the earlier sparse
and separately implemented direct counts. The finite classification is
complete for this fixed subgroup. The proof of minimality is the
injectivity and absolute-sum argument above, not an inference from
failing to find shorter expressions. Independent-author review and
Lean verification remain outstanding. No new Lean job was launched.

## 6. The higher-energy route checked before this classification

The published higher energies in Shkredov's Theorems18 and27 concern
repeated differences, denoted E_k^+, rather than the sum-equality count
T_k^+ used here. The [source's definitions](https://arxiv.org/html/1705.09703v1)
make the distinction explicit. Those bounds cannot be substituted as
higher sum-moment bounds merely because both are called energies.

Two direct extensions do not supply a stronger input. First, decomposing
an H-invariant set Q contained in F_p^*, of size m, into multiplicative cosets and using the
Fourier L^(2r) triangle inequality gives

    E_r(Q) <= (m/n)^(2r) E_r(H).

At r=3, the existing [MRSS bound](https://arxiv.org/html/1712.00410v1)
therefore gives only E_3(Q)<<m^6 n^(-2) log n. It does not establish the
stronger m^5/n estimate that could improve a tripling recurrence.

Second, for centered moments C_s=E_s-n^(2s)/p, Cauchy--Schwarz and
the pass29 recurrence give

    C_(s+1) <= sqrt(C_(2s) C_2)
             <= sqrt(D) n^(s-1/4) sqrt(C_s C_2).

With the known E_2 exponent49/20 this supplies the deficit update
d_(s+1)>=41/40+d_s/2. It is already a consequence of doubling and
interpolation, both included in the earlier closed envelope. These
observations do not rule out stronger higher-energy arguments; they
explain why the specific direct substitutions checked here do not help.

## Remaining uniform problem

Knowing that a short relation is generated by triangles does not mean
it has a short triangle derivation. In this actual quartic-window field,
even six surviving terms can require dozens of triangle equations and
many cycles. A proposed upper-bound proof based on a few local triangle
patterns must control these additional relations too.

This is a finite obstruction to that shortcut, not an infinite lower
bound on derivation length or a counterexample to an unspecified
cubic energy bound. The number of short multiples in arbitrary relevant
ideals, the required growing-order moments, and every full goal remain
unbounded by this analysis.
