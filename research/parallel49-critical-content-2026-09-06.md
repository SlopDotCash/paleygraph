# Critical content, valuations, and nonsplitting primes

The full Paley graph conjecture and Proximity Prize remain unproved.
This pass makes the arithmetic certificate more precise and proves its
odd prime support is confined to the splitting class. That class contains
the target primes, so no uniform bound there follows. Exact checks through
order 128 give no numerical improvement over the preceding certificate.

## 1. Use all coefficients of the existing resultant polynomial

For a dyadic n>=8, write d=n/4 and F=R_n for the primitive polynomial
from the earlier [fiber construction](parallel2-subgroup-2026-09-04.md).
Pass 48 already introduced

    Z_n(T)=Res_Y(F(Y),F'(Y)+T F''(Y)).

Let Ccrit_n be the positive gcd of the integer coefficients of Z_n.
Set Ccrit_4=1. The constant and leading coefficients of Z_n are the two
resultants defining G_n in [pass 48](parallel48-collision-eliminants-2026-09-06.md).
They are nonzero, since F is irreducible over Q and its derivatives have
smaller degree. Consequently

    Ccrit_n divides G_n.                                      (1)

At any odd prime p, reduce in a finite field containing all n-th roots
of unity. If lambda_i are the d labeled roots of F there, then

    Z_n(T)=product_i [F'(lambda_i)+T F''(lambda_i)].

A product of polynomials over a field is zero only if a factor is zero.
At a simple root F' is nonzero; at a double root F'' is nonzero because
2 is invertible; at a root of multiplicity at least three both vanish.
Thus

    p divides Ccrit_n  iff  F modulo p has a triple root.       (2)

There is no requirement p>d in (2). The earlier evaluation criterion
used that restriction to obtain d+1 distinct values of T; using the
coefficients avoids it. The converse for the gcd of only two separate
resultants is still not assumed.

## 2. The exact valuation formula

Lift all n-th roots of unity to an unramified extension of Q_p, using
the unit derivative of X^n-1. Normalize the valuation by v(p)=1.
The corresponding roots lambda_i of F are integral and distinct in
characteristic zero. The coefficient valuation of a polynomial is the
minimum valuation of its coefficients. This valuation is additive on
products: after removing those powers of p, the reductions of the two
remaining primitive polynomials are nonzero, so their product is nonzero.
Applying this elementary Gauss-valuation argument to the linear factors
of Z_n proves

    v_p(Ccrit_n)=sum_i min(v(F'(lambda_i)),v(F''(lambda_i))).    (3)

The extension is unramified, so this is the ordinary valuation of the
rational integer Ccrit_n, without a residue-degree multiplier.

Let c_(r,b) denote the multiplicities of the lambda-values modulo p^r,
and put

    Y_(n,r)=sum_(c_(r,b)>=3) c_(r,b)(c_(r,b)-2).

For each labeled root set

    A_i=sum_(j!=i) v(lambda_i-lambda_j),
    M_i=max_(j!=i) v(lambda_i-lambda_j),
    S_i=sum_(j!=i) 1/(lambda_i-lambda_j).

Since F'(lambda_i) is the product of these differences and
F''(lambda_i)=2F'(lambda_i)S_i, equation (3) becomes

    v_p(Ccrit_n)=sum_i [A_i+min(0,v(S_i))].                 (4)

The ultrametric inequality gives v(S_i)>=-M_i. Also

    A_i-M_i=sum_(r>=1) max(c_r(i)-2,0),

where c_r(i) is the size of root i's class at precision r. Hence

    sum_(r>=1) Y_(n,r) <= v_p(Ccrit_n) <= v_p(G_n),         (5)

and the exact difference on the left is

    sum_i [M_i+min(0,v(S_i))] >= 0.                       (6)

The sums are finite because the roots are distinct in characteristic
zero. A unique closest neighboring root cannot create the correction
in (6), since its reciprocal term has strictly smallest valuation.
Cancellation among several closest reciprocal terms can create it.
Therefore the content valuation is not automatically an exact sum of
the triple-collision masses at all precisions.

## 3. Triple roots force a splitting prime

Let p be any odd prime and n>=8 dyadic. Put

    f_s=ord_s(p),  H=mu_n in F_(p^f_n),
    K=mu_(n/2),  L=H\K=gK.

The same inverse-pair argument applies over this splitting field, since
the n-th power map still has kernel mu_n. Thus the largest root
multiplicity of R_n is the largest value of
1_K*1_L. If f_n=2f_(n/2), the field generated by H is quadratic over
the field generated by K, and {1,g} is a basis. A representation a+gb
with a,b in K then has unique coefficients. Every fiber has size at most
one. This is the earlier field-degree descent, now used with (2).

It remains to consider p not congruent to 1 modulo n for which the
field degree does not double. The elementary order formulas in the
[dyadic descent note](dyadic-descent-and-mixed-energy.md) show that the
common field is F_(p^2) and either

    p=-1 mod n,  or  p=n/2-1 mod n.

Indeed, for n=2^nu and p=1 mod4, f_n=2^max(0,nu-v_2(p-1)); a
nonsplitting step doubles. For p=3 mod4, one has
f_n=2^max(1,nu-v_2(p+1)); the nondoubling cases are exactly the two
listed residues. The order-four case has a degree-one polynomial and
is immediate.

In these two quadratic cases the norm to F_p is 1 on K and is a fixed
epsilon in {1,-1} on L. Its value is 1 when p=-1 mod n and -1 in the
other case. Suppose x=a+b with a in K and b in L. Then x is nonzero,
because -1 belongs to K and the two cosets are disjoint. Set z=a/x and
N=Norm(x). The two norm equations give

    Norm(z)=1/N,   Tr(z)=1+(1-epsilon)/N,

so z satisfies the monic quadratic

    z^2-[1+(1-epsilon)/N]z+1/N=0.                         (7)

There are at most two possible z and hence at most two representations.
When epsilon=1 the two quadratic roots exchange a and b. Only one
ordering can belong to K x L, so there is at most one representation.
It follows that

    p not congruent to 1 mod n implies max_b c_b<=2,
    p divides Ccrit_n, p odd implies p=1 mod n.            (8)

This is a restriction on the prime support of the critical content,
not an estimate at primes already in that support. In particular the
target splitting primes remain completely eligible for triple fibers.

## 4. Two checks against stronger, unjustified interpretations

The first example shows why all coefficients can be needed. For

    F(Y)=Y(Y-5)(Y-1)(Y-4),
    Z(T)=57600-524416T^2+336400T^4,

the gcd of the constant and leading coefficients is 400, while the
coefficient content is 16. Modulo 5 there is a double root and two
simple roots, but no triple root. Thus the separate-resultant gcd has
a false prime factor that the content removes.

The second example shows why (5) can be strict. For

    F(Y)=Y(Y-5)(Y-35),
    Z(T)=-27562500+4622500T^2+520000T^3,

the content is 2500 and its 5-adic valuation is 4. The three roots agree
modulo 5 and separate modulo 25, so the entire triple-mass sequence is
(3,0,0,...). The derivative valuations at the three roots are

| Root | v_5(F') | v_5(F'') | Contribution to (3) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 5 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| 35 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

These are auxiliary integer polynomials, not actual subgroup examples.
They rule out the stronger general algebraic assertions, without ruling
out further identities special to R_n. No such additional identity is
proved here.

## 5. Consequence for the remaining target and exact checks

At the target splitting primes, (5) permits replacing the valuation of
G_s in the sufficient tower criterion of pass 48 by that of Ccrit_s.
This is potentially a weaker arithmetic hypothesis. It still requires
the uniform bound

    sum_(s=4,8,...,N) (3/4)^(log_2(N/s)) v_p(Ccrit_s)/s
        << N^(1/3),

or a sufficient estimate directly on the first-precision masses Y_s.
Neither is established. The prime-support theorem excludes no target
prime, and the elementary height estimate still has the previous power
loss. Thus the uniform energy and triangle exponents do not improve.

The [checker](../experiments/parallel49_critical_content.py) reconstructs
Z_n over the integers for n=8,16,32,64,128, using finite differences
and one extra resultant evaluation beyond the interpolation points.
In all five cases Ccrit_n=G_n exactly, so this calculation produces
no numerical improvement over the earlier finite certificates.
Seventy p-adic checks against the previously certified prime factors
verify (3), the sum over precisions, and the correction in (6), at
verified precision up to 16. In these particular cases the higher
triple masses and derivative corrections are all zero. This finite
agreement does not establish either vanishing uniformly.

Twenty-one nonsplitting field cases cover degrees two, four and eight:
nine field-degree doublings, five equal-norm cases and seven opposite-norm
cases. All 10,920 balanced pairs, 9,984 norm-quadratic evaluations and
primitive root checks agree with (7)-(8). Both auxiliary polynomial
examples are also verified exactly. Full integer coefficients, root
valuation histograms and field checks are in the
[results](../results/parallel49_critical_content_2026_09_06.json).
