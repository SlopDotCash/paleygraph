Determine the leading universal moment contribution and a rigorous Hasse-supported arithmetic-sensitivity bound. Retain finite-n falling factorials; do not turn a short scaling fit into an asymptotic theorem.
2. **Cover-certificate barrier.** Enumerate exact maximum joint agreement M on the saved small hard pencils. Every affine-track partition block has size at most M. Minimize the root-count cap over every possible such partition, so a failure can rule out the certificate family rather than just a particular discovery heuristic. Compare low-rate fixtures with rate1/4 near half-agreement.
3. **Overlapping-support preflight.** Optimize a selected catalog of verified supports S_j so every s-subset intersects some S_j in at least k points. This forces each qualifying codeword onto a selected track by root uniqueness. The completeness logic already exists in the local complete-basis/ZDD tools; the missing prototype selects a smaller catalog using actual overlaps. Verify a hard toy input against the frozen full baseline and measure the covering-number cost. A covering design is known mathematics, and an exponential catalog would not solve the full-scale tooling gap.

These tests decide which information and certificates are worth extending. A failed prototype should produce a precise obstruction or a revised representation, rather than another unsupported proof claim.
