# Prior art and bounded novelty claim

Covariance, shared-randomness couplings, Gram matrices, orthogonal projection and the fact that marginal laws do not determine a joint law are established mathematics. The Rademacher reference calculation is squarefree-monomial orthogonality. None is claimed as an invention.

The local predecessor is [round7](../../round7/README.md): exact single-edit moments, the deletion derivatives, their norms and the internal transform V. Earlier local observable work already retained incidence after unlabelled statistics failed, and earlier spectral work already used common-mode and affine controls. The present output is a specific executable extension: the full derivative Gram matrix R, exact covariance for edits sharing an insertion, four actual finite witnesses, and a streamed implementation that retains the selected input through q=6,700,417.

The new capability is narrower than inventing a general mathematical method. It shows that two non-affine Paley inputs can have identical flattened edit laws and identical collections of per-deletion laws while having distinct shared-insertion covariance, including after projection off the common deletion mode. The matrix is calculated without materializing the full edit table. A simpler per-insertion histogram also distinguishes the toys; no uniqueness or minimality of the covariance statistic is claimed.

Primary literature inspected includes [Döbler, Normal approximation via non-linear exchangeable pairs](https://alea.impa.br/articles/v20/20-08.pdf), which connects reversible generators, conditional increment products, finite-population statistics and spectral decompositions; and [Döbler and Peccati, Quantitative CLTs for symmetric U-statistics using contractions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.00394), which establishes substantial prior art for contraction operators in one- and multi-dimensional U-statistic analysis. No approximation theorem from either source is claimed for these Paley inputs.

Searches on September5,2026 included `Paley deletion covariance`, `Paley insertion Gram`, `Johnson graph conditional covariance`, `U-statistics contraction covariance`, and the exact title of the Döbler–Peccati paper. The narrow Paley searches returned unrelated uses of names and terms, so they provide little novelty evidence. The broader searches establish related machinery. The preceding [round7 source audit](../../round7/local_edits/prior_art.md) records the existing local drift, variance and contraction work.

A useful arithmetic tool and finite information-loss witness have been produced. Historical originality of this exact package remains unestablished; an internet search cannot prove that nobody has ever tried it. The reference-sign model is not an actual conference-matrix construction, and its asymptotic ratio is not a Paley theorem. Neither prize is addressed as a proof target.
