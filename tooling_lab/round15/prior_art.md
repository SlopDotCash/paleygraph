# Primary-source audit

## Arc partitions are established mathematics

Gabriela Araujo-Pardo and Leonardo Martínez-Sandoval, [The arc chromatic number for Galois projective planes, affine planes and Euclidean grids](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19043v1), January26,2026. Read the introduction, definition1.1, and sections2 and4 on September6,2026. They study partitions into sets with no three collinear points, including exact arc chromatic numbers for projective planes and explicit affine-plane partitions using parabolas. They also use a convexity and incidence counting lower bound. Their discussion connects higher-dimensional arcs to MDS codes.

This directly overlaps the geometric core here. It rules out describing partitioning into arcs, determinant checks, or convex size bounds as a newly invented mathematical approach. Our present objective additionally has coordinate multiplicities, a query-avoidance threshold, unqueried coordinates and the cost sum binomial(block_size,d). Those distinctions define our implemented problem; they do not establish historical novelty of that problem or its solution. Local swaps and strict potential descent are also ordinary algorithmic ingredients.

## Conditioning and affine-space pruning are established too

Vikrant Ashvinkumar, Mursalin Habib and Shashank Srivastava, [Algorithmic Improvements to List Decoding of Folded Reed-Solomon Codes](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.12548v1), August18,2025; [SODA2026 proceedings](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1.9781611978971.35). Rechecked sections1.1 and4.1 and the primary author metadata on September6,2026. They describe coordinate-conditioned affine subspaces, earlier KRSW pruning, and deterministic pruning for constant-dimensional affine spaces. Their algorithms and analyses use dimension reduction and affine subspace incidence; these mechanisms precede the conditional-cover idea at the end of this round.

A direct implementation comparison remains open. The proposed word-dependent histogram split is a concrete certificate design and engineering experiment, not a claim to have invented affine conditioning. The verified current contribution is the specific polynomial obstruction, attained finite complete-block optimum, repair evidence and actual decoding measurements. No search or source audit proves global originality.

The Johnson-bound and covering-system references in [round14's audit](../round14/prior_art.md) continue to apply. Neither prize is being proved, and no author or prize body has been contacted.
