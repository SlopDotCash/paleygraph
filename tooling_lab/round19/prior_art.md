# Prior art and scope of the current additions

Research checked September6,2026. The identities and certificate arguments are proved directly in [DERIVATION.md](DERIVATION.md). No historical first claim is made.

- The [round18 audit](../round18/prior_art.md) identifies Heuberger–Katti–Prodinger–Ruan2005 as direct prior art for alternating signed digits and binary-difference transducers. Round19's sharp window-depth tests are a specific finite antiperiodic comparison. Mike Boyle's [author-hosted paper, section2](https://math.umd.edu/~mboyle/papers/aftfinal20sept04.pdf) gives the standard definitions of finite-type constraints, block codes and sofic shifts. These concepts are established, and the present window-versus-memory distinction must not be presented as their invention. No theorem about commuting shifts from that paper is used.
- Daniel Thuerck, Boro Sofranac, Marc E. Pfetsch and Sebastian Pokutta, [Learning Cuts via Enumeration Oracles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.12197),2023, abstract checked. Learning separating inequalities from enumeration oracles is existing research. The present portfolio experiment and explicit dual slack certificate do not invent separation or cutting-plane methods. Its obstruction is scoped to the specified L1-derived relation bounds; it does not establish failure of arbitrary learned cuts.
- Wittawat Kositwattanarerk, Soon Sheng Ong and Frédérique Oggier, [Construction A of Lattices over Number Fields and Block Fading Wiretap Coding](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.2904),2014, abstract checked. It treats constructions from totally real and CM fields and applications to algebraic lattice coset encoding. Reduction homomorphisms and lattice/code interfaces therefore have substantial prior art. We do not apply its coding theorems to the Paley section.
- A primary search excerpt from Frédérique Oggier and Jean-Claude Belfiore's [Enabling Multiplication in Lattice Codes via Construction A](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/server/api/core/bitstreams/d97ff290-b5e6-4e58-bb15-ec666567703e/content) describes componentwise reduction modulo a prime ideal and inverse images of finite-field codes. The repository fetch returned403, so only the search excerpt's introduction was available. No full-paper theorem is imported.

Within this project, [round17](../round17/README.md) already has the complete inverse characterization, and [round18](../round18/README.md) has an exact special digit language. The earlier [geometric lift](../../research/geometric-lift-and-alias.md) and [pass40](../../research/parallel40-shell-converse-2026-09-06.md) already use antiperiodic carries and sparse norm compression. A focused search of the pass40–44 notes found no calibration/re-encoding formula; that limited absence does not prove novelty.

The concrete additions here are a sharp finite-window hierarchy; a subgroup-dependent certificate that excludes an entire bounded-L1 relation interface; an explicit factor-two bracket for a separating relation's coefficient size; and a general scalar-projection/re-encoding implementation with a degenerate fallback. These give different algorithmic consequences from merely collecting more norm or digit-height statistics. They do not prove a prize statement, a global norm estimate, or that no one has previously used these ideas.
