# Local-edit prior art and contribution boundary

The search on September5,2026 covered the existing local research first, then primary mathematical literature. It cannot establish that an idea has never been privately tried, and no historical uniqueness claim is made.

The most relevant local predecessor is [parallel24 classical exceptions](../../../research/parallel24-classical-exceptions-2026-09-05.md). It already derives the exact drift of the sixth power moment M6, uses `||Sh||²=q||h||²-(sum h)²` in a local insertion-variance bound, and proves that a particular thin-shell persistence argument does not remove the exceptional set. That note keeps the difficulty in higher local moments. The present tool computes an exact local second moment of the squarefree elementary target T_d and retains the internal contraction needed to exclude the selected coordinates. The transform identity and the idea of local variance are pre-existing.

[Round2 exchange spectra](../../round2/observables/README.md) already computes exact swap means, Johnson projections and complete global harmonic energies. [Round4 marked moments](../../round4/marked_moments/README.md) already calculates conditional second moments when a few points are fixed, and reduces its three-mark relation data to one contraction. The present conditioning keeps n-1 of the actual n labels for each deletion. Its new executable object is the deletion-derivative contraction V, the exact excluded-insertion correction and the actual equal-histogram/different-local-variance witness. This is a narrower implementation and information-loss result than inventing local conditioning.

Primary sources inspected:

- [Reinert and Röllin, U-statistics and random subgraph counts: multivariate normal approximation via exchangeable pairs and embedding](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.3425) develops exchangeable-pair embeddings and conditional increment calculations for U-statistics and graph statistics. This establishes substantial prior art for the general strategy of retaining additional statistics to close local calculations.
- [Döbler, Normal approximation via non-linear exchangeable pairs, ALEA20(2023)](https://alea.impa.br/articles/v20/20-08.pdf) connects exchangeable pairs, reversible generators, conditional squared increments, spectral decompositions and finite-population statistics. Sections1–2 explicitly supply the generator/carré-du-champ framework. The current tool does not claim to invent it or to establish any of that paper's approximation hypotheses for Paley inputs.
- [Spielman, Spectral and Algebraic Graph Theory](https://www.cs.yale.edu/homes/spielman/sagt/sagt.pdf), section7.3, derives the Paley sign identity `S²=qI-J`. The present calculation uses that standard identity.

That is a search result, not proof of nonexistence elsewhere.

The candidate contribution is useful arithmetic tooling: an exact streaming API for all one-swap means and variances at selected actual sets, a second implementation based on weighted Gram matrices, and a finite witness identifying a specific missing correlation. Any stronger originality or asymptotic statement requires further research and outside review.
