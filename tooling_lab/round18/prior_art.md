# Source audit and novelty scope

Read September6,2026. The specific membership and transfer constructions below are proved directly in [DERIVATION.md](DERIVATION.md); the cited literature establishes prior art, not an imported theorem solving either prize.

- Clemens Heuberger, Rajendra Katti, Helmut Prodinger and Xiaoyu Ruan, *The alternating greedy expansion and applications to computing digit expansions from left-to-right in cryptography*, Theoretical Computer Science341,55–72,2005. Read the [author-hosted paper](https://www.math.aau.at/heuberger/publications/pdf/alg1.pdf), introduction and section2, especially Algorithm1. It already gives alternating nonzero signs and a digit-difference transducer from binary bits. Its balanced expansion uses opposite first and last nonzero signs; our antiperiodic section uses equal first and last signs, hence odd support. The difference transform and alternating language are established machinery. The endpoint and cofactor selection must be stated explicitly; changing them does not establish historical novelty of an arithmetic application. Publication metadata is also available in the [author institution's record](https://tugraz.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/the-alternating-greedy-expansion-and-applications-to-computing-di).
- Clemens Heuberger, Sara Kropf and Helmut Prodinger, *Analysis of Carries in Signed Digit Expansions*. Read the [author-hosted preprint](https://www.finanz.math.tugraz.at/~prodinger/carries.pdf), introduction, digit definitions and sections3–4. It analyzes signed-digit carries with transducers and regular-language probability models. Its symmetric base2 expansion is non-adjacent form, distinct from the present alternating condition. Transfer matrices, digit dependencies and carry counting cannot be claimed as new general mathematical tools.
- Karma Dajani, Cor Kraaikamp and Pierre Liardet, *Ergodic properties of signed binary expansions*,2006. [Publisher abstract](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2006.15.87) checked. It studies classical signed binary expansions through an odometer and a three-state Markov chain, with block-function cocycles. We did not apply its spectral results.

The local [geometric lift and alias](../../research/geometric-lift-and-alias.md) already studies Q=a^N+1, antiperiodic carries and cofactor congruences. Its existing experiment counts additive word relations by moment depth. [Pass40](../../research/parallel40-shell-converse-2026-09-06.md) already contains the exact f=2-X^-1 compression and the five-digit example at a1. The present prototype counts every realizable centered-coset digit support, certifies a complete minimum-support section, and connects alternative saved encodings by exact signed permutations. These are concrete implementation and finite-enumeration additions to this project, not a claim that signed-digit numeration or these applications have never been tried elsewhere.

The minimum-support result concerns a specified family of centered vectors. It is not a shortest-vector result for the whole ideal, a minimum field-norm theorem, or a general Paley/Proximity bound. A full arithmetic support census at one prime does not resolve the worst-case quantifiers of either prize.
