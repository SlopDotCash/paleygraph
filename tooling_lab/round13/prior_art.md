# Prior-art audit, September 6, 2026

| Primary source inspected | Relationship to the prototype |
|---|---|
| [Pikhurko, Constructions of Turán systems that are tight up to a multiplicative constant, Advances in Mathematics 464 (2025), author PDF](https://pikhurko.github.io/E/Pikhurko25am.pdf), introduction | Defines an r-uniform query family contained in every s-set as a Turán system and reviews classical constructions. Our covering objective and partition reasoning belong to this established setting. We additionally allow only evaluation-matroid bases and export certificates for concrete polynomial spaces; no new extremal Turán bound is claimed. |
| [Ashvinkumar, Habib and Srivastava, Algorithmic Improvements to List Decoding of Folded Reed-Solomon Codes, arXiv v1 (2025)](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.12548v1), sections 1.1 and discussion | Already gives deterministic pruning of constant-dimensional affine spaces for general linear codes and discusses algebraic derandomization. Our finite block certificate does not establish a new general deterministic decoder or resolve that broader research direction. |
| [Füredi and Gyárfás, Covering t-element Sets by Partitions, 1991, author-hosted PDF](https://www.renyi.hu/~gyarfas/Cikkek/55_FurediGyarfas_CoveringTElementSetsByPartitions.pdf), introduction and definitions | Studies partitions whose blocks contain each t-set. This is related covering language but has a different containment direction from requiring each large set to contain one small query. It is not cited as the exact theorem used here. |
| [van der Pol, Walsh and Wigal, Turán densities for matroid basis hypergraphs, arXiv v2 (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03673v2), abstract | Studies the maximum number of bases under excluded uniform-minor constraints. The title overlaps our terminology, but its stated extremal objective differs from selecting a small basis family that hits every large coordinate set. Full theorem-level comparison was not performed. |

Candidate contribution: a cheap, verifiable finite cover for actual polynomial spaces, a precise alternative to minimum-density certificates, and complete implementation tests. The 2,070-query and 10-query certificates are explicit new artifacts in this lab. Their global historical originality and optimality are unestablished.

Search phrases included “Turan systems every subset construction”, “matroid Turan bases covering”, and “matroid basis hitting sets derandomization list decoding partition independent triples”. Positive matches constrain claims. Failure to find an identical implementation does not prove that no one tried it.
