# Prior-art and source scope

The current project record already contains [real-subfield recovery](../../research/parallel41-shell-arithmetic-recovery-2026-09-06.md), a centering counterexample, and a [real-unit near-isometry obstruction](../../research/parallel41-shell-unit-distortion-2026-09-06.md). This round does not claim to discover the distinction between Euclidean length and algebraic norm. Its carry experiment concerns the restricted centered coset section, which the near-isometry result explicitly leaves open. We read the pass42 summary as well: its exceptional-prime budget and affine-smoothing recovery comparison do not supply a pointwise shell bound.

[Jacob McNamara, A Bound on the Norm of Shortest Vectors in Lattices Arising from CM Number Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.7840),2012. Abstract checked September6,2026. It introduces weighted quadratic norms, relates minimal-length vectors to field norms through units, and studies a finite set controlling minimal vectors in principal ideals. This overlaps any generic proposal to balance embeddings or relate short vectors to algebraic norm. We have not checked its full theorem hypotheses against this coset section and do not apply a theorem from the abstract.

[Paul Kirchner, Algorithms on Ideal over Complex Multiplication order](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.09037),2016. Abstract checked September6,2026. It extends Gentry-Szydlo methods to CM orders, including polarized ideals, norm equations and real-suborder reductions. These are established arithmetic algorithms. They must not be presented as a newly invented route based on the present scalar norm data; polarized norm information is richer than a single integer field norm.

[Gaohao Cui, Jianing Li and Jincheng Zhuang, Principal ideal problem and ideal shortest vector over rational primes in power-of-two cyclotomic fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07511v2),January2026. Abstract checked September6,2026. Its precise prime-ideal results distinguish congruence classes3,5 modulo8 and7,9 modulo16. Our split primes lie in a different regime. The abstract supports a prior-art warning about shortest generators versus shortest ideal vectors, not an applicable bound for our inputs.

[Klaus Schmidt, Representations of toral automorphisms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.03154v2),2015. Abstract checked September6,2026. It surveys algebraic symbolic representations of toral and compact-group automorphisms using homoclinic points, tracing the approach to earlier work. Recording carries for a centered section is consistent with established symbolic-dynamics machinery. We do not claim a new general coding theorem or apply expansiveness assumptions to these unit maps.

The expanded local audit also found the exact identity (2-X^-1)F=pC and Norm(C)=641*1217 already in [pass40, section3](../../research/parallel40-shell-converse-2026-09-06.md), including the five nonzero digits of C. Thus even the specialized sparse compression formula and one finite example precede this round. [Geometric lift and alias](../../research/geometric-lift-and-alias.md) already uses base-a digits, signed carries with antiperiodic boundary, and the cofactor (a^N+1)/p to recover prime energies. A new digit-language implementation must compare against this existing work rather than rebrand it.

The live frontier was read through [pass43](../../research/parallel43-pass-summary-2026-09-06.md) and the [pass44 structured-level note](../../research/parallel44-structured-levels-2026-09-06.md). They concern exceptional-prime budgets and a sufficient structured-incidence hypothesis. Their reported arguments were not rebuilt here and do not supply a general digit-realizability or norm estimate.

Norm multiplicativity, resultants, lattice reduction, short kernel relations and fundamental-domain carries are all prior-art ingredients. The concrete additions in this round are finite unit-carry witnesses, checked encoding and inverse machinery across the saved inputs, and a complete counterexample census for norm-energy membership summaries. Search found no reason to assert global originality, and no negative search could prove it.
